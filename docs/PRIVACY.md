# Bearing Live - Privacy Policy

_Draft v0.16 · Effective: [date of first App Store release] · Not legal advice: have it reviewed before release._

The Bearing Live apps ("the app") are made by **Bearing** (Texas; formation pending) ("we"). The app is a music app for worship bands. We collect as little as possible. **We do not sell data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.**

## What stays on your iPad (we never receive it)
- Your songs, charts, setlists, arrangements, tones/patches, settings, practice tracks, backing-track stems and rehearsal recordings.
- **The CCLI song-usage log** (which songs your band used, when, and whether lyrics were projected or charts used). It stays on your device. It's only shared if you export it and share the file yourself. Bearing Live does not send anything to CCLI.
- Backups you export go wherever you choose to save or send them (Files, AirDrop, email).

## Band sync on your local network
When you lead or follow a band session, the app sends the setlist, charts, section and tempo to other iPads **on your local Wi-Fi network only**. It also sends each device's name, battery level and temperature state, so the leader can see who's connected. None of this goes through our servers. Backing-track audio never leaves the leader's iPad.

## Subscriptions (Apple)
Apple handles payments. We never see your card or your Apple ID email. The app checks your subscription status with Apple on your device.

## Church teams (only if you use a Church plan)
If you sign in to use one subscription across all Bearing Live apps, or create or join a church team, the app uses **Sign in with Apple** (we don't ask for your name or email) and talks to our seat service. The service stores:
- a scrambled (keyed hash) form of the anonymous Apple user identifier, used for your account;
- your team, the display name you enter, your role, and when you joined;
- the team's App Store subscription transaction identifiers and status, to tell whether the church's plan is active;
- the App Store subscription transaction identifiers, product, which Bearing Live app sold it, and its status (active / renewed / expired / refunded), so one subscription can unlock every Bearing Live app;
- short-lived invite codes (stored hashed) and rate-limit counters.

**Free-trial history:** to keep the 2-week free trial to one per person, we keep a **hashed (scrambled) record of your Apple ID identifier** along with a hash of the subscription that carried the trial and the date. **We keep this record even after you delete your account**, so a deleted and re-created account can't get a second trial. It can't be turned back into your Apple ID or your email.

You can delete your account in the app (Settings > Subscription > Church team > Delete account). That removes your account, linked subscriptions, membership and team data (it does not cancel the Apple subscription - do that in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions), but not the hashed trial record above. To ask about your data, contact [support email].

## Children
The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
